NovaTech Solutions - Support Guide

How to Contact Support:
- Email: support@novatech.io
- Live chat: Available inside the NovaTech Workspace dashboard (bottom right corner)
- Emergency line: For critical production outages, email support@novatech.io with "URGENT" in the subject

Response Time SLAs:
- Starter plan: Response within 2 business days
- Growth plan: Response within 8 business hours
- Enterprise plan: Response within 1 hour, 24/7

Before Contacting Support:
1. Check the status page at status.novatech.io for known outages
2. Search the help center at help.novatech.io
3. Restart the app or clear your browser cache
4. If the issue is with an integration, check that your API token has not expired

Submitting a Bug Report:
Include the following in your report: your account email, the browser or app version, steps to reproduce the issue,
and a screenshot or screen recording if possible. Bug reports without reproduction steps may be deprioritized.

Escalation:
If your issue has not been resolved within the SLA window, reply to your existing ticket with "ESCALATE" in the subject.
Your ticket will be assigned to a senior support engineer.
