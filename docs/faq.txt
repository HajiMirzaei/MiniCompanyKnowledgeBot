NovaTech Solutions - Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What does NovaTech Solutions do?
A: NovaTech Solutions is a B2B software company that builds cloud-based project management and team collaboration tools for mid-size engineering teams.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the login page and click "Forgot Password". Enter your company email and you will receive a reset link within 5 minutes.
If you don't receive it, check your spam folder or contact support@novatech.io.

Q: What are your support hours?
A: Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm CET. For critical production issues,
we have 24/7 emergency support via support@novatech.io with "URGENT" in the subject line.

Q: Is NovaTech's platform available on mobile?
A: Yes. NovaTech has iOS and Android apps available. They support core features including task management, notifications, and document viewing.
Full editing is available on desktop.

Q: How do I invite a new team member?
A: Go to Settings > Team > Invite Member. Enter their email and select their role (Viewer, Editor, or Admin).
They will receive an invitation email valid for 48 hours.
