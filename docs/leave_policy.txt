NovaTech Solutions - Leave Policy

Annual Leave:
All full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave on a pro-rata basis.
Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the following year. Carried-over days expire on March 31.

Sick Leave:
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave does not carry over.

Parental Leave:
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Leave can begin up to 2 weeks before the expected birth date.

Public Holidays:
NovaTech follows the public holiday calendar of the employee's country of residence.
Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a replacement day off within 30 days.

How to Request Leave:
Submit leave requests through the NovaTech HR portal at hr.novatech.io at least 5 business days in advance for annual leave.
Sick leave should be reported to your direct manager by 10am on the first day of absence.
