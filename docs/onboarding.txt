NovaTech Solutions - New Employee Onboarding

Week 1:
- Day 1: Receive laptop and access credentials from IT. Complete security awareness training (mandatory, 2 hours).
- Day 2: Meet your direct manager and team. Review your 30-60-90 day plan.
- Day 3-5: Shadow a senior team member. Get access to all relevant systems (HR portal, NovaTech Workspace, GitHub org, Slack).

System Access Checklist:
- NovaTech Workspace (hr.novatech.io will send invite)
- GitHub organization (request via your manager)
- Slack workspace: novatech.slack.com
- Google Workspace (email and Drive)
- 1Password team vault for shared credentials

Probation Period:
All new employees have a 3-month probation period. A formal review is held at the end of month 3.
Early confirmation is possible if performance criteria are met. During probation, the notice period is 2 weeks on both sides.

Equipment Policy:
NovaTech provides a MacBook Pro (M-series) or equivalent for all employees.
You may request peripherals (monitor, keyboard, mouse) through the IT portal within your first 30 days.
Equipment remains company property.

- HR questions: hr@novatech.io
- Office manager: office@novatech.io
